Handler shortcuts require active-handler state. Paired dog shortcuts are contextual to active dog role; medicine and Vet placement can restrict actions. Source registers unified shared key commands to avoid duplicated legacy handler/dog mappings. Your saved client bindings still need checking after upgrading.

Adoption, service approval, progression and active selection are separate concepts. A handler can own multiple dogs; only the selected active dog is the normal command/feedback target. Civil ranks are independent of service levels. Training is cooperative and does not remotely pathfind the player dog.
