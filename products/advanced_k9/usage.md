# Advanced K9 — Usage

1. Open /k9 (F7), obtain service-role approvals if needed and invite a nearby second player (8 m default).
2. Dog accepts and controls its dog ped. Enable Active K9 handler before using handler shortcuts.
3. Select the intended active dog in the team roster when owning multiple dogs.
4. Train obedience/fetch and operational skills. Formal drills require dog acceptance; orders are advisory.
5. Use operations for scent/sniff/manual alerts, vehicle for cages and care for bowls/petting/treats.
6. Adopt for persistent character ownership/naming/collar details; inspect passport and specialization.

Service levels: Recruit 0 XP, Nosework I 15, Tracker 35, Search Specialist 60, Patrol K9 90, Elite K9 140. Sniff/fetch unlock at 2, tracking at 3, buried search at 4, tackle/bite at 5. Civil progression is separate: police-style sniff/tracking/tackle defaults disabled, bite enabled at civil rank 5.

Positive detections can remain dog-private until manual alert. Water/vehicles break scent by default; actual marker color is pink. Dog/animal peds have infinite stamina even unpaired. /k9food and /k9water place bowls; repeated overfeeding can trigger dump. Framework needs are synchronized.

Values are supplied defaults; administrators may customize them. Source: current config and registered gameplay handlers.
