# Advanced Government — Usage

Open /government or City Hall. Elections move through registration, campaign, voting and finish; defaults are 48/72/24 hours and 14-day term. Background advancement runs around once a minute. Candidate deposit is 50,000; party creation 25,000. Donations fund campaign balance; advertisements charge that balance.

Mayor assigns cabinet permissions. The government job alone is not current mayor/cabinet membership. Business bosses apply for grants/bid on tenders; private concessions also need registered NRP ownership. Government reviews/awards with ledger transfers. Laws, budgets and referendums use their own authorized workflows.

Tax keys: income/business/vat/vehicle/property/capital. Rates alone do not automatically tax other resources; ordinary wage tax needs the external QBox hook.

Values are supplied defaults; administrators may customize them. Source: current config and registered gameplay handlers.
