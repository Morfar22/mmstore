# Advanced Car Radio — Usage

Open /carradio or F7 in an allowed vehicle, paste a playable URL and manage personal playlist/vehicle library/queue. Now Playing handles shared volume, transport, loop and seek. Personal listener settings affect only your own listening (own vehicle/other vehicle/on foot). Closing UI does not stop the radio.

Passengers can control by default; RequireDriverToControl and DriverOnlyOpen are separate. ESC closes. Outside leakage: closed .20, one open/broken window .55, +.12 per extra up to .90; door/roof/open class 1.00. Distance/listener volumes apply separately. Saved plate state may resume when the vehicle is used again after restart.

Values are supplied defaults; administrators may customize them. Source: current config and registered gameplay handlers.
