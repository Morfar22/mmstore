# Advanced Diving — Usage

1. Open dock UI or /diving and invite by ID as leader (four max, 35 m range).
2. Select an XP-unlocked contract, use shared boat and dock scuba toggle.
3. Complete pickup/cut/recover targets. Lift requires bag attachment, surfacing then boat loading within 14 m.
4. G (/divingsonar) briefly reveals nearest unfinished target; 15 s cooldown.
5. Finish all objectives and return assigned boat within 16 m of marina point.

Each online member receives full base pay plus 5% per extra member, capped 15%, and full contract XP. Pay is not split. Leader leaving cancels active run; nonleader departures remove them. Runs are not resumed from DB.

Contracts: Harbour Cleanup level 1 / 3,200 / 180 XP; Lost Cargo level 3 / 5,200 / 310; Wreck Expedition level 7 / 7,600 / 470; Deep Water Salvage level 12 / 11,500 / 700.

Values are supplied defaults; administrators may customize them. Source: current config and registered gameplay handlers.
