# Get useful support quickly

Include:

- Product version and mm_bridge version.
- Selected framework, inventory, target, phone/billing and their versions.
- mmbridge_status output and relevant F8/server errors.
- Steps to reproduce, expected result and actual result.
- Solo/co-op role, location and whether it happens after reconnect or provider restart.
- Relevant config excerpt with passwords, tokens and connection strings removed.

For payment issues include operation/character/invoice IDs and confirmed provider transaction records. For prescriptions include item name, slot and relevant prescription metadata. For K9/Vet include the dog/handler/patient roles and placement IDs.

Use your MM – Store customer support channel. No Discord invitation or storefront URL is invented in this package.
